## Role

As a **Staff Site Reliability Engineer** at Prodigy Education, I own the reliability, scalability, and day-to-day operations of Prodigy's infrastructure on AWS. This spans everything from incident response and capacity planning to long-term architectural roadmaps that I present to engineering leadership.

## Responsibilities

- **Platform operations** — monitoring, alerting, and on-call for Kubernetes workloads running on AWS EKS
- **Infrastructure direction** — evaluating new tools and services, writing proposals, and driving adoption across engineering teams
- **Documentation & enablement** — producing runbooks, architecture decision records, and onboarding guides for developers

## Recent Projects

- **Artifact management migration** — led the transition from ECR and NPMjs to a self-hosted JFrog Artifactory instance, reducing external dependency and improving supply-chain security
- **Secrets management modernization** — replaced legacy Kubernetes External Secrets with External Secrets Operator, standardizing how teams access secrets from AWS Secrets Manager and Parameter Store

## Stack

Kubernetes (EKS) · AWS (EKS, EC2, RDS, S3, IAM, Secrets Manager, CloudWatch) · Terraform · Helm · JFrog Artifactory · Datadog · GitHub Actions
